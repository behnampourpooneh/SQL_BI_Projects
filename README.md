# Sales & Business Intelligence Analysis — AdventureWorksDW 2019

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is a comprehensive SQL-based Business Intelligence analysis using the **AdventureWorksDW 2019** dataset.  
The goal is to extract actionable business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and operational efficiency.

The analysis focuses on transforming raw transactional data into meaningful KPIs for decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- SQL (T-SQL)
- Microsoft SQL Server
- AdventureWorksDW 2019 Dataset

---

## 📊 Key Business Analyses

### 1. Sales Performance Over Time
- Analyzed total sales by year using shipping date.
- Identified overall revenue trends.

### 2. Product Category Performance
- Ranked top-selling product subcategories.
- Identified high-performing product segments.

### 3. Sales Contribution Analysis
- Calculated each product category's share of total sales.
- Evaluated revenue distribution across categories.

### 4. Profitability Analysis
- Computed profit per transaction.
- Calculated profit margin for each sale.
- Identified high and low profitability transactions.

### 5. Monthly Sales Trends
- Analyzed monthly sales patterns for 2012–2013.
- Identified seasonality trends in revenue.

### 6. Sales Employee Performance
- Measured total sales per employee.
- Evaluated performance across different territories.

### 7. Customer Acquisition Analysis
- Identified new customers based on first purchase date.
- Analyzed acquisition trends over time.

### 8. Customer Segmentation (Top Customers)
- Ranked top 10 customers per country/region.
- Identified high-value customers.

### 9. Profit Contribution by Employees
- Evaluated employee contribution to total profitability.
- Combined HR and sales performance metrics.

---

## 🚚 Delivery & Logistics Analysis

### 10. Average Delivery Time
- Calculated average time between order date and shipment date.

### 11. Delayed Shipments
- Identified orders shipped after the due date.

### 12. Freight Cost Analysis
- Analyzed average freight cost per country/region.
- Evaluated logistics cost distribution.

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- Sales performance varies significantly across product categories.
- A small group of customers contributes to a large portion of revenue.
- Delivery delays exist and should be further investigated for optimization.
- Profitability is not always aligned with revenue, highlighting cost impact.
